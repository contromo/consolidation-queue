\section{Limitations}

The noisy-mode support is mechanism-local. CQ separates from \reflection{} on
forced contradiction and partially on preference drift under the current 32B
artifacts, ties \memzero{} on the frozen sentinel, and leaves
source-independence, scope-contamination, useful-pending, and poisoning-defense
rows unsupported under the current evidence.

The LongMemEval result is also bounded. The completed transfer probe is stable
and negative for base CQ on evidence completeness. The pending multi-evidence
follow-up supports a readout-cardinality repair for that v1 loss. LongMemEval-S
is not evaluated here: the 2026-05-25 feasibility gate failed because no local
source artifact was available to pin, so no held-out adapter or policy run is
authorized in this revision. LongMemEval-V2, distractor-heavy retrieval
quality, and natural-language QA success remain future evaluation targets; the
\code{answer_correct} surface is diagnostic-only in the transfer probe because
policy answers are trace-shaped.

PFLC is a diagnostic class rather than a new pass/fail gate. The field-level
survey records what released baseline artifacts expose. LoCoMo's evidence
field is a clean gold-side target, and MemoryAgentBench's conflict-resolution
tasks are PFLC-relevant. The survey outcome splits: most released
artifact contracts and standard output surfaces are blocked for PFLC, while
the AMB LoCoMo subset admits a published-output replay that decomposes
aggregate accuracy without resolving the blocked majority.

The resolution audit replay repair remains an abort. The
lookup diagnostic leaves the aborted audit and the noisy same-stream policy
comparison's mixed-support verdict unchanged. A subsequent fresh archived
replay was executed under the publication-hardening preregistration
\S5 and is documented as Bucket \code{B_inconclusive_min_n}: zero
alias-CQR hits surfaced for \splitcode{useful\_\allowbreak pending\_\allowbreak memory}
or \splitcode{memory\_\allowbreak poisoning} under the locked alias function
and the 32B extractor cell, leaving the cross-tab of lookup hit versus
per-policy answer success non-evaluable under the preregistered minimum-N gate.
Prior PFLC null rows for those families remain unattributed, and Claim 2's
null-row attribution is unchanged. The full readout and skip carve-out are
recorded in \artifact{docs/publication_hardening_step3_results.md} and
\artifact{docs/publication_hardening_step3_precondition_skip.md}.

Finally, all experiments are local-first prototypes. Real-user weeks-long
helpfulness, learned semantic scope inference, production retrieval stacks, and
API-hosted extractors remain future evaluation targets. A PFLC-surface deletion
experiment also remains future work: the current local policies do not expose
pre-existing separable policy-facing query surfaces that would support such an
experiment without refactoring. The source package and branch-pinned
reproduction path for the present claims are recorded in
Appendix~\ref{app:reproducibility}.
