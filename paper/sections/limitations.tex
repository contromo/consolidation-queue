\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{CQ support is narrow.} The noisy-mode support is limited to named
mechanisms. CQ separates from \reflection{} on forced contradiction and
partially on preference drift under the current 32B artifacts, ties the
\memzero{} baseline on the frozen sentinel, and leaves source-independence,
scope-contamination, useful-pending, and poisoning-defense rows unsupported.
A stronger extractor could produce CQ separations, policy ties, or CQ
weaknesses. This revision leaves the outcome for those rows open.

\item \textbf{Scenario co-design remains a threat to external validity.}
Same-stream fairness controls policy inputs and storage within a comparison,
but it does not remove the fact that CQ, the protocol, and the oracle scenarios
were designed in the same project. The external AMB LoCoMo replay is therefore
important evidence about released benchmark artifacts, not a replacement for
running more independently designed policy suites.

\item \textbf{LongMemEval v1 is bounded and cardinality-sensitive.} The
controlled v1 probe is stable and negative for base CQ on evidence
completeness, and the pending multi-evidence follow-up supports a
change to the answer-time read interface for that v1 loss. The v1 oracle split
used here is evidence-only, and the scorer-only random floor shows why
returning two evidence sessions is enough in this cell. LongMemEval-S,
LongMemEval-V2,
distractor-heavy retrieval quality, and natural-language QA success remain
future targets.

\item \textbf{LoCoMo covers returned evidence ids only.}
The AMB LoCoMo replay shows when published contexts contain the gold evidence
ids, and separates those cases from rank effects and downstream answer
residuals. It does not certify that a memory policy can name its own
governable evidence handle. The internal canonical-id diagnostic tests that
separate question.

\item \textbf{PFLC diagnoses artifact surfaces.} The field-level survey
records what released baseline artifacts expose. Most released artifact
contracts and standard output surfaces are blocked for PFLC replay, while the
AMB LoCoMo subset admits a published-output replay that decomposes aggregate
accuracy without resolving the blocked majority.

\item \textbf{The ADD/UPDATE/NOOP baseline is narrow.} The local baseline is a
rule-based comparator over the shared candidate stream and substrate.
Production Mem0 exposes richer add, search, update, delete, and platform
behavior, so this paper's results characterize only the local comparator.

\item \textbf{Deployment surfaces remain outside this evaluation.} The experiments do not
measure real-user weeks-long helpfulness, learned semantic scope inference,
production retrieval stacks, API-hosted extractors, deployment latency,
deletion, or compliance. A future deletion experiment first needs separable
lookup surfaces and registered leakage metrics.

\item \textbf{The resolution audit remains inconclusive.} The lookup diagnostic
leaves the aborted resolution audit and the noisy same-stream policy
comparison's mixed-support verdict unchanged. A later archived replay surfaced
too few alias-resolution hits to evaluate the lookup-versus-answer-success
cross-tab under its preregistered minimum-count rule. Prior PFLC null rows for
those families remain unattributed. The archived readout is recorded in
Appendix~\ref{app:reproducibility} and Appendix~\ref{app:artifacts}.
\end{itemize}
