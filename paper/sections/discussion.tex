\section{Discussion}

The result is a contingency map. When the noisy stream preserves contradiction
edges and the policy-query surface stays aligned, CQ's contestation and
demotion mechanism remains visible. When the stream or interface does not
expose canonical slots, source identity, scope handles, or the relevant
policy-facing identifier, policy ties are not evidence that the policies are
equivalent. They are evidence that the current benchmark surface failed to
expose the distinction.

This is why the negative and mixed rows are central rather than embarrassing.
The 7B gate shows that aggregate component metrics can hide concentrated
failures. The 32B Bucket B audit shows that even a valid unlock does not turn
every tie into convergence. The QR-canon audit shows that clustering-quality
metrics can pass perfectly while exact lookup handles fail. The LongMemEval
transfer shows that an external adapter can pass methodology gates and still
produce a stable CQ-negative result. The pending multi-evidence follow-up then
shows how a preregistered interface repair can isolate one failure mechanism
without rewriting the original result.

The strongest claim is therefore methodological. Same-stream memory-policy
evaluation makes policy distinctions auditable. It can show positive mechanism
survival, such as forced contradiction under 32B extraction. It can also show
when a policy distinction is hidden by the interface, such as exact
policy-query canonical-id failure under perfect clustering or evidence
completeness failure under singleton pending readout. A useful benchmark must
make both outcomes visible.

The CQ case study also illustrates why immediate-write baselines must be strong
and fair. Reflection wins some rows for mechanistically uninteresting reasons,
such as overwriting into a durable state that happens to include both evidence
sessions. That is still a valid comparison result. The proper response is not
to weaken the baseline or change the metric after seeing the loss; it is to
name the mechanism, preregister a repair if warranted, and add a control that
can reproduce the failure by changing only the suspected interface.
